FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about connecting, gameplay, donor ranks, and community rules.

Open Minecraft and navigate to Multiplayer. Click "Add Server", enter play.BeaconMC.space as the Server Address, and connect! Bedrock players can also join using the same IP.
Both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition connect using the exact server address: play.BeaconMC.space.
Yes, BeaconMC proudly supports Bedrock players! Connect using play.BeaconMC.space with Bedrock port 19132. Check our Discord for support and updates!
Ranks are purchased exclusively through our Discord! Click any "JOIN DISCORD TO BUY" button, join our official Discord server, and open a "Buy Ranks Ticket" in our support channel.
We accept UPI, PhonePe, Google Pay, and Paytm directly inside our Discord tickets. There is no web checkout.
Support is readily available 24/7 on our Discord server. You can ask in our help channels or open a dedicated support ticket.
Our staff team can be reached directly through the BeaconMC Discord server via ticket or direct chat. You can view all our active leadership and staff members on our Staff page.
Yes! We host regular community events, building challenges, seasonal contests, and adventure activities announced in our Discord.
The actual server version is 1.21.11. With ViaVersion, ViaBackwards, and ViaRewind installed, Java Edition players can connect on versions from 1.7.10 up to 26.3! Bedrock is also supported.
Yes, BeaconMC is 100% free to join and play! All core features and survival gameplay are accessible to every player. Ranks are purely optional aesthetic and utility perks that help support server hosting costs.
