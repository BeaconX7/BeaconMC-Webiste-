COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

SERVER RULES

Please follow these rules to ensure a fun, safe, and fair experience for everyone.

01

Respect Everyone

Treat all players and staff members with courtesy and kindness. Toxicity is not tolerated.

02

No Cheating

Hacked clients, x-ray packs, combat aids, or unapproved modifications are strictly prohibited.

03

No Exploiting

Do not abuse bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics. Report discovered exploits to staff immediately.

04

No Griefing

Do not destroy, vandalize, or alter structures, builds, or terrain belonging to other players.

05

No Harassment

Bullying, hate speech, threats, and discrimination will result in an immediate and permanent ban.

06

Follow Staff Instructions

Staff decisions are made to preserve network integrity and fairness. Cooperate with staff requests.

07

No Advertising

Do not promote other servers, websites, services, or personal social media without permission.

08

Have Fun

Relax, build great things, embark on adventures, and make unforgettable memories with friends!

WITNESSED A RULE VIOLATION?

Our moderation team handles player reports with screenshots or recordings in our Discord support tickets.
