COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

SERVER RULES

Please follow these rules to ensure an enjoyable experience for everyone

01

Respect Everyone

Treat all players and staff members with courtesy and kindness. Toxicity is not tolerated.

02

No Cheating

Hacked clients, x-ray packs, combat aids, or unapproved modifications are strictly prohibited.

03

No Exploiting

Do not abuse bugs, glitches, or unintended mechanics. Report discovered exploits to staff immediately.

04

No Griefing

Do not destroy, vandalize, or alter structures, builds, or terrain belonging to other players.

05

No Harassment

Bullying, hate speech, threats, and discrimination will result in an immediate and permanent ban.

06

Follow Staff Instructions

Staff decisions are made to preserve network integrity and fairness. Cooperate with staff requests.

07

No Advertising

Do not promote other servers, websites, services, or personal social media without permission.

08

Have Fun

Relax, build great things, embark on adventures, and make unforgettable memories with friends!

Everything you need to know about joining and playing on BeaconMC

Both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition connect using the exact server address: play.BeaconMC.space.
Yes, BeaconMC proudly supports Bedrock players! Connect using play.BeaconMC.space with Bedrock port 19132. Check our Discord for support and updates!
Ranks are purchased exclusively through our Discord! Click any "JOIN DISCORD TO BUY" button, join our official Discord server, and open a "Buy Ranks Ticket" in our support channel.
We accept UPI, PhonePe, Google Pay, and Paytm directly inside our Discord tickets. There is no web checkout.
Support is readily available 24/7 on our Discord server. You can ask in our help channels or open a dedicated support ticket.
Our staff members (Owner: BeaconX7, Founder: AshuX7, Co Owner: Echo_X1, Admin: _ishaaaaaaa, Staff: RoronoaZORO_18) can be reached directly through the BeaconMC Discord server via ticket or direct chat.
Yes! We host regular community events, building challenges, seasonal contests, and adventure activities announced in our Discord.
